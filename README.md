## ~/ product-case-studies

### 01. FirstClub — Increasing 30-Day Repeat Purchase

**Problem:** How can FirstClub increase the percentage of first-time customers who place a second order within 30 days?

**What I did**
- Analyzed **1,185 Play Store reviews**
- Used AI-assisted thematic analysis to identify recurring customer signals
- Identified four behavioral first-time-user segments
- Mapped discovery, trust, convenience and repeat-purchase drivers
- Investigated the path from first order → repeat behavior

**Focus:** Product Analytics · Customer Research · Segmentation · Retention

[→ GitHub: App Review Pulse](https://github.com/gakshat415-lang/App-Review-Pulse)

---

### 02. Myntra — Wishlist → Purchase Conversion

**Problem:** Increase the percentage of users who purchase at least one wishlisted item within 30 days.

**What I did**
- Analyzed **4,447 public conversations**
- Filtered to **927 Myntra-relevant conversations**
- Applied AI / qualitative analysis
- Identified **6 opportunity themes**
- Decomposed the wishlist-to-purchase funnel across relevance, consideration, decision and transaction

**Focus:** User Research · Product Analytics · Funnel Decomposition · Opportunity Identification

[→ Research tooling: NL Internet Scraper](https://github.com/gakshat415-lang/NL-INTERNET-SCRAPER)

---

### 03. ChatGPT — Voice Adoption for India's Next Billion Users

**Problem:** Vernacular job seekers can be comfortable with typing but may struggle when interacting through Voice Mode because of interruption and cognitive pressure.

**Product proposal:** **Paced Mode**

A user-controlled voice interaction model that gives users control over when recording starts and ends.

**What I explored**
- User segmentation & journey mapping
- Behavioral friction in voice interactions
- Push-to-talk interaction model
- PRD-style product requirements
- KPI and launch-readiness thinking
- Working prototype

**Focus:** Product Strategy · User Research · AI Products · UX

---

### 04. Uber — Pickup Reliability in High-Density Urban Hubs

**Problem:** Traditional 2D GPS coordinates can fail to represent the physical reality of dense locations such as airports, business hubs and multi-level infrastructure.

**Product proposal:** **Spatial OS**

A street-aware pickup experience using:
- Semantic Landmark Chips
- 3D Spatial Alerts
- Visual / Color Handshake
- Reduced coordination burden between rider and driver

**Focus:** JTBD · First Principles · TAM/SAM/SOM · RICE Prioritization · Service Reliability

---

### 05. Spotify — Collaborative Playlists

**Problem:** Collaborative Playlists sound inherently social, yet they struggle to become a regular behavior.

**What I explored**
- Why setup friction kills spontaneous collaboration
- Lack of contribution feedback
- Social risk around music sharing
- Differences between Collaborative Playlists, Blend and Jam
- JTBD-driven product improvements

**Focus:** JTBD · First Principles Thinking · Behavioral Psychology · Product Experience
